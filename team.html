About the team

The riders behind the tradition.

Escaramuza Ameyali was founded in 2019 by South Bay families passing on the love of Mexican escaramuza. Every rider trains year-round — and every performance they give funds the foundation’s work with children.

Melany Lara

17 · Riding since age 10

Charrería has become my passion. It has given me a second family, unforgettable memories, and taught me the value of dedication, teamwork, and pride in our traditions.

Daniela Batres

17 · 9 years with Ameyali

Escaramuza has been my passion since I was 8, and it has shaped me into the person I am today. It taught me to be a problem solver and a team player. My horses are Duvalin and Luna.

19 · Riding since age 9

My passion for horses and my dedication to escaramuza have helped me develop confidence, discipline, and a deep appreciation for this cherished tradition.

Ximena Jimenez

16 · Riding since age 5

This is my second year as an escaramuza and as part of Ameyali. I can’t wait to see what next season has in store for me!

Julissa Munoz

17 · Riding since age 8

Being an escaramuza taught me to be fearless. I grew up afraid of animals — horses changed that completely. Riding has become a form of therapy and a passion, and I love teaching young children to ride.

Valentina Sabino

16 · Riding since age 5

I’m passionate about riding because it’s a way to be free from everyday life. I love being around horses.
